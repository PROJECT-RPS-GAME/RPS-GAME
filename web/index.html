HOW TO PLAY

ROCK
closed fist
PAPER
open hand, fingers out

index + middle finger
  • Press Start round. After 3 - 2 - 1 - GO! show your sign and hold it still.
  • 2 players: Player 1 stands on the left half of the camera, Player 2 on the right.
  • Menus: move your hand to guide the pointer, then pinch thumb + index to click a button. Holding still will not click.
  • Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
